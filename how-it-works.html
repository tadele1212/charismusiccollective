HOW IT WORKS

Follow 5 simple steps from registration to starting your first official live music lesson.

1

Submit Registration Form

Fill out our simple multi-step registration form choosing your preferred instrument/program, experience level, learning format (Online or In-Person), and preferred weekly schedule.

2

Receive Registration ID & Payment Details

Upon registration, you will receive a unique Registration ID (e.g. CMC-2026-00101) and bank/Telebirr transfer account details on the confirmation page.

3

Send Receipt via Telegram

Complete your transfer and send a screenshot of your payment receipt along with your Registration ID directly to our official Telegram account: @Iyasu_Markos.

4

Admin Verification & Status Approval

Our administration verifies your transaction against bank records and officially updates your status from Pending Payment to Approved Student.

5

Schedule Confirmation & Classes Begin!

Instructor Iyasu Markos contacts you directly to confirm your exact class timetable and sends your live online video link or studio address for your first session.
