ABOUT CHARIS MUSIC COLLECTIVE

Empowering musicians, worship leaders, and audio creators with world-class personalized music education.

WHO WE ARE

Building The Next Generation of Musicians

Charis Music Collective is a modern music education institution and creative hub founded by Iyasu Markos. We specialize in practical, high-impact music training for students across Ethiopia and internationally.

Whether you are picking up an instrument for the very first time, preparing for church worship performance, or refining your studio mixing and arrangement skills, our personalized one-on-one curriculum gives you the exact tools to excel.

Our Mission

To nurture musical passion into real-world technical skill, confidence, and artistic originality through practical one-on-one instruction.

METHODOLOGY

OUR TEACHING APPROACH

We reject rigid, dull theoretical drills in favor of dynamic, practical musical growth.

Customized Student Centered

Every student learns at their own pace. We evaluate your current starting level and build a custom roadmap aligned with your musical goals.

Practical Real-World Focus

You learn chords, grooves, and arrangements by playing actual songs, worship tracks, and studio productions rather than dry abstract exercises.

Direct Instructor Feedback

Receive immediate, constructive technical feedback on hand placement, rhythm, posture, and tone during live one-on-one sessions.
