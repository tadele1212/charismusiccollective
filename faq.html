FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about joining Charis Music Collective.

What instruments and programs do you teach?

We offer specialized programs in Piano, Acoustic & Electric Guitar, Bass Guitar, Music Theory & Tutorial, Music Arrangement, Mixing & Mastering, and Audio Production.

Do you offer online classes?

Yes! We offer live, one-on-one interactive online classes accessible worldwide. Lessons feature HD video streams, multi-camera angles of the keyboard and guitar fretboard, and digital lesson sheets.

Do you offer in-person classes?

Yes, in-person sessions take place at our music studio in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. Students get direct physical guidance, access to studio amplifiers, keyboards, and acoustic treatment.

Are absolute beginners welcome?

Absolutely! A large portion of our students start as complete beginners with zero prior music experience. Lessons are taught step-by-step at a comfortable, encouraging pace.

How long is each lesson and how often?

Standard regular sessions run 60 minutes per lesson, scheduled on a weekly basis according to your chosen preferred days and time slot.

How do I pay and send my payment receipt?

Payment can be made via Bank Transfer (CBE) or Telebirr. After transferring the fee using your Registration ID (e.g. CMC-2026-00101) as reference, take a screenshot of your receipt and send it to our official Telegram handle: @IyasuMarkos.

When is my registration officially approved?

Your registration is officially approved as soon as our administrator verifies your payment receipt against bank records. You can check your registration status anytime using our Check Status page.

Can I change my preferred schedule or instrument later?

Yes. If your work or school schedule changes, simply reach out to your instructor via Telegram or phone to adjust your weekly lesson times slot.
